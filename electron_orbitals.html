Amplitudes are normalized so Σ|ck|² = 1, and each component evolves in time as cke−iEkt with relative phase set at t = 0. In a multi-state superposition, electrons follow the full 3D Bohmian guidance velocity Im(∇ψ/ψ) + A via adaptive RK4 integration, and time scaling is capped when energy splittings ΔE are large so interference beats remain watchable. The field acts through weak-field Zeeman and Larmor terms (the B² diamagnetic term is neglected). During a single-state photon transition, the cloud moves by an interpolated flow rather than the guidance equation.
