The field slider is scaled to the orbital: at 1, the Larmor rotation equals the circulation of an m = 1 electron at 0.3 of the cloud radius. The readout shows the real field. During a transition the cloud is moved from the old density to the new one by an interpolated flow (each electron keeps its place in the cumulative radial and polar distributions), and the circulation blends from m to m′. It lasts a few seconds here instead of the real ~10⁻⁸ s, and it is not a solution of the guidance equation. The density overlay is hidden until it ends. The field acts through the weak-field Zeeman and Larmor terms (the B² diamagnetic term is neglected).
